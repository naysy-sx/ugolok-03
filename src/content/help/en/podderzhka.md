# Support and contact

Ugolok is made by its author and developer — and I read everything you send.

Write to **[support@ugolok.tech](mailto:support@ugolok.tech)** if:

- you have a question about how something works and the help doesn't answer it;
- you'd like to suggest an idea or an improvement;
- you found a bug or something doesn't work as described — tell me what you did, what you expected and what happened (and in which browser or on which device);
- you noticed an inaccuracy in the help or in a translation of the interface;
- you have trouble reaching the server, or simply want to share feedback.

**Important:** never send your secret phrase, password or private key (`nsec...`) — support doesn't ask for them and doesn't need them.
